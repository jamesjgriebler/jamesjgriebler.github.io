CV & Bio

James J. Griebler, Ph.D.

B.S., Bioproducts and Biosystems Engineering, University of Minnesota Twin Cities, 2020
B.S., Mathematics, University of Minnesota Twin Cities, 2020
M.S., Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, 2024
Ph. D., Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, 2024
Postdoc, University of Tennessee Knoxville, 2025-Present

Jimmy develops new ways to study soft materials across fields, space, and time using the tools of recovery rheology.
In addition to research, he is passionate about the history of rheology, and digitizing the early work of the field. Under Resources you can find some early works when available, or links to find others.
He enjoys cheering for the Minnesota Vikings, playing tabletop card games, and spending time with his wife and two cats (Booker & Chip).
